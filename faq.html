Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about Puffora Naturals, our products, orders, shipping, and more.

Are your products 100% natural?

Yes. Our dry fruits, dates, nuts, and seeds are carefully sourced from trusted growers and selected for their premium quality, freshness, and natural taste without unnecessary additives.

How should I store the products?

Store products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. After opening, keep them in an airtight container to maintain freshness and flavor.

Do your products contain preservatives?

We prioritize natural quality. Product-specific ingredient information is provided on each product page and packaging.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are usually processed within 24–48 hours. Delivery times vary depending on your location and courier service.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Orders can only be modified or cancelled before they are dispatched. Please contact our support team as soon as possible.

Do you accept returns?

Due to the perishable nature of our products, returns are accepted only for damaged, defective, or incorrect items reported within 48 hours of delivery.

Are your products suitable for everyday consumption?

Yes. Dry fruits, nuts, dates, and seeds can be part of a balanced daily diet when consumed in appropriate portions.

How can I contact Puffora Naturals?

You can reach us through our Contact Us page, email, or phone number listed on our website. Our support team will be happy to assist you.
